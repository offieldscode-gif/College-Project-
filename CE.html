🏛️ About Civil Engineering
Civil Engineering deals with the planning, design, construction and maintenance of infrastructure such as buildings, bridges, highways, dams, water systems, transportation networks and smart cities.

It combines engineering science, mathematics, materials, structures and project management to create safe and sustainable infrastructure.
