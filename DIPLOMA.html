📘 About Diploma

Diploma / Polytechnic programs provide practical and technical education in engineering and applied technologies. Students learn through classroom concepts, laboratory work, practical training, projects and industry-oriented skills. These programs can prepare students for technical careers as well as further studies such as B.Tech through suitable pathways.
