💼 About MBA
Master of Business Administration (MBA) is a postgraduate program designed to develop business, management, leadership and strategic decision-making skills.

Students explore areas such as marketing, finance, human resources, operations, entrepreneurship, business analytics and strategic management.
📚 4 Semester Journey

  • Business Communication

  • Quantitative Techniques
