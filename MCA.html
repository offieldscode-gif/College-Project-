🧠 About MCA
Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is a postgraduate program focused on advanced computer applications, programming, software development, databases, networking, cloud computing, artificial intelligence and modern technologies.

MCA prepares students to design, develop and manage modern software systems and technology-driven applications.
